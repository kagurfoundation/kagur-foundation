Who we are

A young Trust, built around five old ideas.

Founded in 2026 and based in Gurugram, Kagur Foundation exists to promote social welfare, human development, and inclusive growth — with a focus on health, education, skill development for students, and the empowerment of women in underserved and marginalized communities.

We are newly established, and we believe the way to earn trust is to be plain about who we are and what we do. Origin story to confirm

In detail

What each pillar means on the ground.

01

Health-Related Activities

What we do
Health check-up, blood, and eye camps; medical awareness, preventive healthcare, sanitation and nutrition drives; maternal, child, adolescent, and mental-health awareness. We help needy individuals access basic medical services, medicines, diagnostics, and treatment — working with hospitals, doctors, and healthcare professionals.
Who we serve
Underserved and economically weaker communities.
How you can help
Sponsor a health campVolunteer as a medical professionalDonate medical supplies
02

Skill Development of Students

What we do
Vocational and technical training, digital literacy, and career-oriented workshops; personality, communication, entrepreneurship, and leadership development; computer education, language and financial literacy, workplace readiness, internship guidance, mentorship, and exposure visits — often with educational institutions, training centres, and industry experts.
Who we serve
Students, particularly from underprivileged backgrounds.
How you can help
Sponsor a student's trainingVolunteer as a mentor/trainerDonate equipment
03

Education

What we do
Scholarships, books, uniforms, stationery, and digital learning materials; support for schools, coaching centres, libraries, and remedial education; literacy and adult/non-formal education; and support for underprivileged children, school dropouts, and differently-abled learners.
Who we serve
Children, youth, and disadvantaged groups in rural and urban communities.
How you can help
Sponsor a student's educationDonate books/materialsVolunteer as a teacher/tutor
04

Women Empowerment

What we do
Awareness on women's rights, legal literacy, health, hygiene, and safety; vocational training and self-employment opportunities; support for self-help groups and livelihood initiatives; and education, counselling, and capacity-building for women and girls.
Who we serve
Women and girls in underserved communities.
How you can help
Sponsor a training programVolunteer as a mentor/trainerSupport a self-help group
